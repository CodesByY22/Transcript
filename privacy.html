Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Simple, plain-English privacy commitment.

🔒 Summary: TranscriptKit does not collect, transmit, share, or sell any of your data. Everything runs 100% locally on your machine.

1. Zero Data Collection

TranscriptKit does not collect or log:

  • Your name, email address, IP address, or any personally identifiable information (PII).
  • Your browsing history, video watch history, or search history.
  • The contents of any video transcripts you copy, search, or export.

We do not operate any tracking servers, analytics engines (such as Google Analytics or Mixpanel), or third-party advertising trackers.

2. 100% Local Execution

All transcript extraction, subtitle parsing, timestamp calculations, formatting, paragraph merging, and file exports are executed strictly in-memory inside your Chrome browser sandbox.

No text or video metadata ever leaves your personal device.

3. Minimal Permissions Justification

Under Manifest V3 guidelines, TranscriptKit requests only the absolute minimum permissions necessary to function:

  • storage: Used strictly to save your preferred export format (Text/SRT/Markdown), language selection, and timestamp preferences locally on your computer via chrome.storage.local.
  • https://www.youtube.com/*: Required solely to read captions on YouTube watch pages and inject the optional "Transcript" button next to Like/Share. The extension cannot access any other website or domain.

4. Third-Party Services

TranscriptKit does not integrate with any external third-party APIs. The only network communications made are directly between your browser and YouTube's official caption endpoints when you actively request a transcript for a video you are watching.
