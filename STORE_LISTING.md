## Detailed Description

TranscriptKit is the fastest, cleanest, 100% free tool to copy, search, and export YouTube transcripts. Designed for students, researchers, content creators, and note-takers.

### ✨ Key Features
- **1-Click Copy & Export**: Instantly copy full transcripts or download as `.txt`, `.srt`, or `.md` (Markdown).
- **Multiple Formats**: Export clean text, SubRip subtitle files (`.srt`), or formatted Markdown with headers.
- **Search Inside Transcript**: Live search with match counter to pinpoint exact spoken moments in seconds.
- **Clickable Timestamps**: Click any timestamp in the transcript to jump right to that moment in the video.
- **Chapter Support**: Automatically groups transcript sections under chapter titles when available.
- **Noise & Filler Removal**: Automatically strips `[Music]`, `[Applause]`, and dedupes repetitive auto-generated speech text for clean, human-readable prose.
- **Paragraph Merging**: Intelligently groups short caption bursts into natural paragraphs based on speaker pauses.
- **On-Page Quick Button**: Adds a minimal "Transcript" button directly under the video near Like/Share for instant access.
- **Quick Keyboard Shortcut**: Press `Alt+Shift+C` (or customize in `chrome://extensions/shortcuts`) to copy the transcript instantly without opening any menus.
- **Theme Matching**: Automatically adapts to your system and YouTube light/dark themes.

### 🔒 100% Free & Completely Private
- **No limits**: Unlimited video transcripts of any length (even 3+ hour podcasts).
- **No accounts or logins required**: Just install and use.
- **Zero data collection**: No telemetry, no analytics, no external servers. Everything runs entirely locally in your browser.
